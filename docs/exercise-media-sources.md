## Matching and captions

- Keep exercise aliases explicit; do not match by substring or use a different apparatus as a generic fallback. The barbell squat intentionally does not claim the generic alias “Agachamento livre”.
- The captions describe what the photographs show, rather than assuming that every pair has the same beginning/end order.
- For the plank, image 0 is preparation on the floor and image 1 is the forearm plank. For the alternating curl, the photographs show the two alternating sides. These are still photos, not a video or a continuous movement recording.
- Visual inspection of all 108 originals was performed in locally rendered contact sheets (the original 60, then all 48 additions). Images show people exercising in gym settings; dimensions and individual SHA-256 hashes are listed below.

- Expanded with 18 common, specifically named variations; no student records were accessed to select them. The ambiguous aliases “Supino com halteres” and “Desenvolvimento de ombros com halteres” were removed so import matching does not infer the bench angle or seated/standing posture. Scott photos use an EZ/W bar, and its aliases say so.

## Additions selected from saved workout names

- Added 24 specifically photographed movements (48 original JPEGs) after reviewing the aggregate exercise-name audit of saved workouts. Selection used movement names and counts only; no student names, contact information or workout history is included in this catalogue or provenance file.
- Added treadmill walking/running, elliptical and stationary bicycle references; reverse machine flyes, standing leg curl, Smith/hack squat, face pull, hip thrust, floor bridges, cable exercises, bench dips and named abdominal variations.
- Treadmill photographs show different camera angles as well as a phase of movement. The stationary-bike pair includes a closer view. Dead bug photographs show arms held up while one leg extends. Hip-thrust photo 0 is lowered and photo 1 elevated; Smith-squat photo 0 is flexed and photo 1 upright. Captions were checked against the originals rather than assuming start/end order.
- Generic cardio does not identify an apparatus. A horizontal leg press, TRX squat or bird dog was not replaced by another movement from this source. Unconfirmed equipment abbreviations such as HBC/HBL do not become photo aliases.
- Existing instructions, timing, isometrics, combinations and machine identifiers remain attached to the prescribed exercise. Reviewed references to a base movement must keep those original details; see `docs/exercise-name-review.md` for alias review.
