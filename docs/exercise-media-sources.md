## Matching and captions

- Keep exercise aliases explicit; do not match by substring or use a different apparatus as a generic fallback. The barbell squat intentionally does not claim the generic alias “Agachamento livre”.
- The captions describe what the photographs show, rather than assuming that every pair has the same beginning/end order.
- For the plank, image 0 is preparation on the floor and image 1 is the forearm plank. For the alternating curl, the photographs show the two alternating sides. These are still photos, not a video or a continuous movement recording.
- Visual inspection of all 60 originals was performed in a locally rendered contact sheet. Images show people exercising in gym settings; dimensions and individual SHA-256 hashes are listed below.

- Expanded with 18 common, specifically named variations; no student records were accessed to select them. The ambiguous aliases “Supino com halteres” and “Desenvolvimento de ombros com halteres” were removed so import matching does not infer the bench angle or seated/standing posture. Scott photos use an EZ/W bar, and its aliases say so.
